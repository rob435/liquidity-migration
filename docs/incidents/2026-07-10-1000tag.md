## What failed

The demonstrated failure was governance and lifecycle, not proof of one missing
stop parameter:

- three demo-only sniper adds increased same-symbol exposure without paper or
  frozen-backtest parity;
- entry sizing had no explicit loss-at-disaster budget;
- shared-symbol exit and Closed-PnL allocation were not sufficiently
  component-aware for reliable forensic ledgers;
- planned exit deadlines and restart recovery were not durable enough across
  every sleeve;
- reconciliation called historical sniper notional “exposure” and local price
  return “realized PnL,” obscuring venue truth.

Simple fixed stops are not the evidence-backed answer. Prior full portfolio
replays of 20%, 40%, and 80% adverse stops reduced MAR on both venues. A short
fade often experiences adverse excursion before reversion. This incident
justifies better ex-ante exposure budgets and executable adverse-state research;
it does not reverse those stop results.
